Mandi price source & benchmarks
Source: Agmarknet (Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India) daily wholesale mandi price index.
  • Grade A 100% modal price: premium export grade (>55mm, uniform skin, 0% rot)
  • Grade B 65% modal price: standard domestic wholesale (40-55mm, minor defect)
  • Small 45% modal price: sambar / pickle wholesale grade (30-40mm)
  • Reject No commercial price (₹0.0): unfit for retail market
